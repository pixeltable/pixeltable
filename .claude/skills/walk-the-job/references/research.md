# Research

Facts outside the product that this job depends on. The session and the brief quote the product. This command is for what the product cannot answer.

Leave the product as you found it. Leave `lifecycle/job.md` as you found it, unless the card file is absent. If it is absent, write it first by [card.md](card.md), then research.

## What to look up

Read the card and [questions.md](questions.md). List the claims the job, done, or leave would depend on that the product does not already say. Typical claims: what this person does today to finish the job, the number a change would assume, the other way they already finish it. "Are there enough of them?" is one of these claims when the job depends on it. The product cannot answer that one.

If the product already says it, quote the product in the table and do not search for a second version.

A claim you cannot tie to this job stays out.

## The table

| # | Claim | Kind | Where |

Kind is `Fact` or `Inference`.

- A Fact has a source you opened: a page, a file, a command's output. Name it.
- An Inference is your reading of a Fact in the table. Point at that row.
- A claim with no source is omitted. Say, under the table, which questions you looked for and could not place.

Save the table to `lifecycle/research.md`. The reply is that table. Do not write the session, and do not write the brief.
