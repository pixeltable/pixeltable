# Research, critic, and brief

Use the existing job card. For research, establish one from evidence if absent. A brief requires a card; a critic pass requires a session or brief. If that prerequisite is missing, identify it rather than inventing a retrospective account.

## Research

Find outside facts that affect this particular person's job, done, or leave. These may include the current alternative, a claimed constraint, a frequency, or a market assumption. Do not search for facts unrelated to a decision. Product behavior is established from the product.

Record claim, kind, source, and implication. A fact has a source opened and checked. An inference points to the supporting fact and stays labeled. Unsupported claims remain open questions. Preserve disagreement between sources. Do not turn a proposed persona into evidence of customer demand.

## Critic

Challenge the existing writeup. Check quotes and locations, invented feelings or actions, a substituted person, generic changes, skipped trouble, and unexamined work after success. Check whether inferences became facts or questions were marked answered without evidence. Check whether a proposed change follows from a break and whether deferral leaves done unreachable.

Report location, problem, and the check or rewrite that would repair it. Leave the product and the reviewed session or brief unchanged. Do not silently repair the evidence before presenting the critique.

## Brief

Use the card, checked product evidence, sourced research, and unresolved critic findings. Preserve their uncertainty. If a session has not been run, say so. Address the questions the evidence can answer:

- Who is the customer, and what establishes that enough such customers exist?
- What makes the problem painful, and is it described concretely?
- Is a fix known, and does it address the actual problem?
- What supports repeat use or customer preference, rather than predicting affection?
- Which change matters most for reaching done?
- Which implementation uncertainties and missing features remain?
- What can be deferred without preventing done?
- What new problems does success create, and which are helped or still open?

A source-based walkthrough cannot prove demand, pain, or customer preference. Separate observed behavior, scenario-based assessment, and outside research. A confident sentence is not evidence.

Give the job, supported answers and open questions, the observed or proposed breaks, work created after success, one recommended change, and the checks needed before building. Do not invent a change outside the collected evidence. Keep other unresolved blockers visible. Follow existing authorization for implementation.

Keep outputs in the response unless persistent artifacts were requested. Then use the chosen location or `lifecycle/research.md`, `lifecycle/critic.md`, and `lifecycle/brief.md` for the respective commands.
